Frequently Asked Questions

Register an account, navigate to the Events page, find an open tournament, and click "Register". Make sure your team meets the eligibility requirements.

Players must be at least 13 years old. Players under 18 require parental consent for prize money collection.

Rankings are based on tournament placement points, match wins, and strength of schedule. Points decay after 6 months of inactivity.

Teams can qualify through our weekly online qualifiers or by invitation. Contact us for more details on team registration.
